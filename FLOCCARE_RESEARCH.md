# FlocCare research and product fit

Research checked on 24 October 2026. This brief uses FlocCare's own pages where possible. Claims about its product are the company's descriptions, not independent validation.

## What FlocCare does

FlocCare is a Singapore-based healthcare AI company. Its site positions the product as a platform for personalized, localized communication for healthcare providers, pharma, insurance, and medical device organizations. It describes content for clinician education, patient education, public health awareness, social channels, blogs, and newsletters. The company emphasizes multilingual content, regional regulatory awareness, and clinical review. [Company site](https://floccare.ai/) · [FAQ](https://floccare.ai/faq)

Its careers page describes a team building AI features for healthcare content and lists engineering, product, data, medical, growth, and marketing roles. [Careers](https://www.floccare.ai/careers)

FlocCare's funding page identifies Shilpa Karkeraa as CEO and Rhythm Jolly as CBO and says the company raised US$500,000 in pre-seed funding. A March 2025 FlocCare press release described planned expansion of disease-specific models and geographic reach. [FlocCare funding page](https://floccare.ai/funding-announcement.html) · [Press release](https://en.prnasia.com/releases/apac/floccare-secures-us-500k-to-drive-healthcare-awareness-and-patient-engagement-with-gen-ai-483279.shtml)

## Design decisions for this assignment

- **Multi-brand instead of FlocCare-only:** The brief asks for repeated use cases, so the generator accepts any company identity and preserves multiple projects.
- **Live brand preview:** A user can see the logo and color before generation, reducing avoidable reruns.
- **Consistent article template:** All pages in a project share typography, navigation, brand identity, and footer.
- **Editorial warning:** Health content is sensitive. The product avoids claiming generated articles are medically approved and asks for human review before sharing.
- **Model separation:** FlocCare describes proprietary healthcare models; this assignment prototype uses general-purpose open models (Gemma by default) through OpenRouter. It does not claim the same clinical grounding or regulatory capabilities.

## Recommended next iteration for a FlocCare-style product

Add authenticated workspaces; source-grounded writing from an approved clinical knowledge base; citations to approved references; reviewer workflow with status and audit trail; region and audience settings; language selection; and controlled publishing after sign-off. These changes would be needed before positioning the tool as a healthcare communication system rather than a general blog generator.
